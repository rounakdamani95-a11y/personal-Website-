01 About

A little about me

I've never really fit into one box and honestly I stopped trying a long time ago. I'm curious about a lot of things, I ask too many questions, and I genuinely enjoy figuring out how people and ideas connect.

This little corner of the internet is just a space for me to share what's on my mind, what I'm up to, and what I believe in. Nothing curated, nothing over-thought. Just me.

If something here feels familiar or sparks a thought, reach out. I love a good conversation.

02 What I Do

Started in sales. Found my home in marketing.

FMCG, broadband, lease lines. Years of being face to face with customers, figuring out what actually makes people say yes. That sales background is what shaped how I think about marketing today.

My honest belief: if you haven't done sales, you're just guessing. Sales teaches you to listen before you pitch. Marketing without that is just noise.

Now I'm exploring how AI can make marketers sharper without making their work feel less human. That's the balance I'm chasing.

03 What I Care About

Things that drive me

01

Going with the flow

The people who adapt are the ones who last. Holding on too tight to how things should be just slows you down. I'd rather bend and keep moving.

02

Real over polished

I'll take one honest conversation over ten impressive ones any day. I don't care much for performance. What you see is what you get.

03

Keep growing

I look back at who I was a year ago and I'm glad I'm not that person anymore. Getting better bit by bit is the whole point for me.

04

People first

The things that have mattered most in my life came from being around the right people. I take relationships seriously.

04 Life Outside the Screen

The things that fill me up

🏏

Outdoor sports

Cricket, football, pickleball. If it's outdoors and there's something to compete for, I'm showing up. Sport has taught me more about people and pressure than anything else.

🎸

Music

Soul, Bollywood melodies that hit you somewhere deep. The kind of music that slows everything down. And on some days a bit of acoustic pop that makes you want to drive with the windows down. I don't just listen. I sing along. Loudly.

🌊

Traveling

I've chased quiet mountain mornings where the fog doesn't lift till noon. Sat on raw, unhurried beaches where nobody was performing for anyone. Found stillness in the hills where the only sound is wind. Every kind of travel teaches me something about what I actually need.
